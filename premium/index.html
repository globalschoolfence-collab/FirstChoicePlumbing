Common questions

Answers before you even call.

Straight answers about pricing, emergencies and how we work. Don't see your question? Just ask.

Call (555) 123-4567 Ask a question
01Do you offer 24/7 emergency plumbing?

Yes. Our emergency line is answered around the clock, 365 days a year, and we aim to have a licensed plumber at your door within 60 minutes. There's no extra charge for nights, weekends or holidays.

02How much will my repair cost?

We diagnose the problem first, then give you a flat-rate price before any work starts. The price we quote is the price you pay, with no hidden fees or hourly surprises. Estimates for new installations are always free.

03Are your plumbers licensed and insured?

Every technician is fully licensed, insured and background-checked, and personally trained by our founder, Kevin. You'll know exactly who is coming into your home.

04Do you guarantee your work?

Yes. Every repair and installation comes with our 2-year workmanship warranty. If something we fixed fails because of our work, we come back and make it right at no charge.

05How soon can you come for a non-emergency job?

Call or send the quote form and we'll schedule you right away. Same-day appointments are often available, and we call back within 30 minutes during business hours.

06What should I do before you arrive in an emergency?

Turn off your home's main water shut-off valve to stop the flooding, and switch off the water heater if the leak is near it. Move valuables away from the water. If you smell gas, leave the house first and call your gas utility or 911 from outside.

07Can you find a leak without tearing out my walls?

In most cases, yes. We use acoustic listening equipment, thermal imaging and camera inspections to pinpoint hidden leaks, so we only open up the exact spot that needs repair.

08Should I repair or replace my water heater?

Tank water heaters usually last around 8 to 12 years. If yours is newer and the problem is a part like a thermostat or valve, a repair usually makes sense. If it's older, leaking from the tank, or rusty, replacement is often the better value. We'll give you honest options for both.

09Do you give second opinions?

Yes, and they're free. If another company quoted you a big repair, we'll take a look and tell you honestly whether it's really needed.

10Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. We handle restaurants, offices and retail spaces, including grease traps, fixtures, backflow testing and scheduled maintenance plans.

11Will you clean up after the job?

Always. Our team uses boot covers and drop cloths, and we clean up completely before we leave.
