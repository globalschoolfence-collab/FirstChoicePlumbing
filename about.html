Our Story

From One Truck to a Team You Trust

First Choice Plumbing & Excavating Co was founded in 2009 by master plumber David Chen with one truck, one mission: to give homeowners a plumber they could actually trust. He was tired of seeing neighbors get overcharged, oversold, and underserved by big franchise companies that cared more about the invoice than the customer.

Today, First Choice Plumbing & Excavating Co has grown to a team of 14 licensed professionals, but that founding principle hasn't changed. Every plumber on our team is personally trained by David, and every job comes with our flat-rate pricing, workmanship guarantee, and the promise that we won't recommend work you don't actually need.
